      Good to know

      Weather

      Cool, 8–13°C (46–55°F). Thursday (Thanksgiving) brings heavy rain and wind: bring a raincoat, not an umbrella. Friday to Sunday look dry with sun and clouds. Sunset is around 4:30pm, so plan the views early.

      Thanksgiving = closed

      On Thursday a lot of shops and some restaurants close or keep short hours. Friday is Black Friday: shops open early and it gets crowded. Check opening hours before you go anywhere on Thursday.

      Subway

      Tap your own contactless card or phone at the gate. About $3 a ride; after 12 rides in 7 days the rest of the week is free. Our line is the L from Bedford Av.

      Going out

      • Be in line before 11pm.
      • Get tickets or a guest-list spot during the day.
      • Bring your passport: 21+ and ID checks everywhere.
